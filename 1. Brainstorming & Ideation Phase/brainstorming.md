# Brainstorming & Ideation Phase

## Project Title

LegalEase: AI-Powered Legal Document Generator

## Problem Statement

Creating legal documents manually can be time-consuming and difficult for users who are not familiar with legal formats.

## Proposed Solution

LegalEase is an AI-powered application that helps users generate draft legal documents by entering the required information.

## Main Idea

The main idea is to provide a simple application where users enter document details and receive a generated draft legal document.

The user can then review, edit and download the generated document.

## Target Users

- Students
- Individuals
- Small businesses
- Users who need basic draft documents

## Possible Documents

The system can be designed to support draft documents such as:

- Employment documents
- Rental or lease documents
- Non-disclosure agreements
- Service agreements
- Business-related documents

## Benefits

- Saves time
- Provides a simple user interface
- Helps generate draft documents quickly
- Allows users to edit the generated document
- Provides downloadable output

## Technologies

- Python
- FastAPI
- Streamlit
- Gemini

## Expected Outcome

The expected outcome is an easy-to-use AI-powered application that can generate draft legal documents based on user-provided information.

## Legal Disclaimer

The generated documents are drafts and should be reviewed by a qualified legal professional before legal use.
