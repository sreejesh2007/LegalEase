# 1. Brainstorming & Ideation

## Project Title

LegalEase: AI-Powered Legal Document Generator

## Idea

The idea is to create an AI-powered web application that helps users generate structured legal document drafts from simple user inputs.

## Problem Identified

Creating legal documents manually can require significant time and knowledge of legal document structures.

Users may find it difficult to understand:

- Required sections
- Important terms
- Document structure
- Formal wording

## Proposed Solution

LegalEase provides a simple interface where users select a document type and enter relevant information.

Gemini AI then generates a structured legal document draft.

## Target Users

- Students
- Small businesses
- Freelancers
- Individuals
- Entrepreneurs
- Users who need general-purpose document drafts

## Initial Ideas

The team considered:

1. Employment agreement generator
2. Lease agreement generator
3. NDA generator
4. Service agreement generator
5. General agreement generator

These ideas were combined into one application.

## Final Idea

The final idea is to build LegalEase as a centralized AI-powered legal document generation platform.
